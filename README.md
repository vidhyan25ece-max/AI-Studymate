# 🎓 AI StudyMate

> Your AI-powered learning companion for smarter and more personalized studying.

## 📌 About the App

AI StudyMate is a GenAI-powered student learning platform that turns study materials into an interactive learning experience. Students can upload their study materials, ask questions, get simple explanations, generate quizzes, and receive examples to understand concepts better.

It also helps students plan their preparation with **personalized study timetables, priority-based topics, and study reports** based on their available time and exam goals.
